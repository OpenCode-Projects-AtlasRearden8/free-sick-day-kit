No-Cook Dinner Rescue Deck

Nine dinners for a day of couch nursing and zero stove gas left in the tank.
Print, cut on the dashed lines, stash cards in a drawer for an emergency-dinner moment. (cut on the dashed lines)

CARD 01
Snack-Board Dinner
Cheese, crackers, cut veggies, nuts, dried fruit. Whatever the fridge offers. A cutting board counts as a plate.
Zero cooking
CARD 02
Rotisserie Chicken + Bagged Salad
The store did the hard part. Shred the chicken, dump the salad in a bowl, dinner's done.
Store handles it
CARD 03
Deli Sandwich Bar
Bread, deli meat, cheese, mustard. Everybody builds their own and the kitchen stays untouched.
Assembly only
CARD 04
Hummus Plate
Hummus, pita or crackers, baby carrots, cucumber slices, cherry tomatoes. Protein and crunch in one.
Open and arrange
CARD 05
Cereal-for-Dinner Night
Cereal, milk, one sliced banana on top. Legally counts as dinner, especially on sick days.
Pour level only
CARD 06
Yogurt Parfait Bar
Yogurt cups, granola, berries or jam, honey drizzle. Layers in a cup make it feel like dessert.
Spoon work only
CARD 07
Tuna or Chicken Salad Wraps
Pouch tuna or canned chicken, mayo, salt, scoop onto tortillas with lettuce. Roll and go.
Tin opens easily
CARD 08
Picnic Abroad
Spread a blanket somewhere new: living room floor, back porch, hallway tent. Same food, new location, kids amazed.
Location hack
CARD 09
Breakfast-for-Dinner Rescue
Toast or bagels, cream cheese, fruit, cold milk. No stove guilt, everyone's favorite loophole.
Toaster only

Free Sick-Day Kit — education and organizing only. Not medical advice. Check allergies and label instructions, and keep cold food cold.
